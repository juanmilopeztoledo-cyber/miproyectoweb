About me

Hey there! I'm Juanmi López, a 23-year-old with a passion for freezing moments in time through the lens. Eight years ago, I stumbled into the captivating world of photography, and it's been a wild ride ever since.

Welcome to my portfolio, where I invite you to explore the visual stories I've captured along the way. From candid moments to carefully composed shots, join me in navigating the chapters of my photographic journey. Let the pixels unfold!
